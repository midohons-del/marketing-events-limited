Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information MARKETING EVENTS LIMITED — the company that owns and operates the Marketing Events Limited website — collects through this site, why it is collected, and what you can ask us to do with it.

01Who is responsible

MARKETING EVENTS LIMITED, registered under Company No. 12480079, with its registered office at RPG The Copper Room, Trinity Way, Salford, England M3 7BG, is the controller of any personal information collected through this website. Marketing Events Limited is a trading brand of MARKETING EVENTS LIMITED; correspondence sent to either name reaches the same company. You can contact us at contact@marketing-events-limited.marketing or +44 161 496 0184.

02Information you give us

If you use the contact form, email us, or call us, we receive the information you choose to send: typically your name, your email address, your telephone number if you provide it, your company, and whatever you tell us about your business or the work you are considering. We do not require you to create an account, and this website does not process payments.

03Information collected automatically

Like most websites, this site and the hosting provider that serves it record standard technical information such as IP address, browser and device type, referring page, requested pages, and timestamps. Where analytics tooling is enabled, it is used in aggregate to understand which pages are read and how visitors arrive. We do not attempt to identify individual visitors from this technical data.

04How the information is used

We use what you send us to reply to your enquiry, to work out whether an engagement makes sense for both sides, to deliver and administer services under a signed agreement, to keep records required for tax and company filings, and to maintain the security and performance of this website. We do not use your information to build advertising profiles about you, and we do not sell personal information.

05Legal grounds for processing

Where the UK GDPR, EU GDPR, the CCPA or a comparable law applies to you, we rely on your consent when you voluntarily contact us, on the necessity of taking steps at your request before entering a contract, on the performance of a contract once you become a client, on our legitimate interest in operating and securing this website, and on legal obligation for record-keeping.

06Sharing with others

We share information only with service providers that make this business run &mdash; email hosting, website hosting, analytics, accounting and, where relevant, advertising platforms used to deliver services under a client agreement &mdash; and only to the extent they need it. Those providers act under contract and confidentiality obligations. We may also disclose information where we are legally required to, or where it is necessary to establish or defend a legal claim.

07How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not become engagements are kept only as long as the conversation is live and for a short period afterwards. Client records are kept for the duration of the engagement and then for the period required by the law of the jurisdiction in which MARKETING EVENTS LIMITED is registered and by our tax and accounting obligations. After that, records are deleted or anonymised.

08Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask for a copy of the personal information MARKETING EVENTS LIMITED holds about you, to have it corrected, to have it deleted, to restrict or object to certain processing, and to receive it in a portable format. To exercise any right, write to contact@marketing-events-limited.marketing. We will respond within the period required by the applicable law, and we will not treat you differently for asking.

09Children

This website is aimed at businesses and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact contact@marketing-events-limited.marketing and we will delete it.

10Security and changes

We apply reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect the information sent to us, though no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure. We may update this policy as the business or the law changes; the version published on this page is the current one.
