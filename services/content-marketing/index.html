Content Marketing

Content briefed around the questions your buyers ask right before they convert. Run in-house by Marketing Events Limited, not outsourced.

Content Marketing at Marketing Events Limited starts with a full audit of what’s currently working and what isn’t — no cookie-cutter checklist, no vanity metrics. We write for the specific questions your buyers ask right before they’re ready to talk to sales, not for generic top-of-funnel traffic.

From there we build a plan specific to your business: a content calendar mapped to your sales stages, briefs written by people who’ve read your competitors’ content so we don’t repeat it, and distribution planned before a single word is written.

Content starts contributing to pipeline instead of just page views, because it was built to move someone toward a decision. We report on the metrics that actually predict revenue, not clicks for their own sake, and we adjust the plan every month based on what the data shows.

In this service

  • Content strategy and editorial calendar
  • SEO-informed content briefs
  • Long-form articles and guides
  • Distribution plan across owned channels
  • Monthly performance and engagement reporting
